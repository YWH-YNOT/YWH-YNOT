## Engineering Principles

- Distinguish build success, board execution, and physical acceptance.
- Use versioned protocols with sequence, CRC, heartbeat, timeout, and fault semantics.
- Separate real-time control from complex business and display workloads.
- Support claims with source, tests, logs, versions, and explicit evidence boundaries.

I am currently deepening my work in real-time embedded systems, model quantization and deployment, robot state estimation and motion control, and ROS 2.
